The UK government announced major reforms transitioning from the legacy non-dom regime to a modern residence-based foreign income and gains (FIG) regime. Under the updated rules, individuals arriving in the UK can benefit from a 4-year exemption on foreign earnings before becoming subject to UK tax on worldwide income.
