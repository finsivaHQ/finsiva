**Urban Immovable Property Tax (UIPT)** is administered by provincial Excise and Taxation Departments (Punjab, Sindh, KPK, Balochistan, and ICT). Rates vary depending on rating area categories (A, B, C), property usage (self-occupied vs rented), and property size.
