## Definition

Benefit-in-Kind (BiK) company car tax is the tax charged on the financial value of a corporate vehicle provided by an employer for employee personal or private use.

## Explanation

For electric company cars, HMRC sets extremely low BiK percentage rates to encourage zero-emission transport:
- **2024/25:** 2% BiK rate
- **2025/26:** 3% BiK rate
- **2026/27:** 4% BiK rate
- **2027/28:** 5% BiK rate

The taxable benefit amount equals the car's official P11D price multiplied by the BiK rate. The employee pays tax on this benefit amount at their marginal Income Tax rate (20%, 40%, or 45%). Employers pay Class 1A National Insurance at 15% on the benefit amount.
