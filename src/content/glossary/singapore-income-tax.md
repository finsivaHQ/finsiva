---
term: "Singapore Income Tax"
category: "Income Tax"
definition: "Singapore income tax is a territorial tax system administered by the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS). Resident individuals pay progressive personal income tax rates ranging from 0% to 24% for Year of Assessment 2026, while companies pay a flat headline corporate tax rate of 17% with generous partial tax exemptions. Singapore imposes 0% capital gains tax and zero dividend withholding tax."
