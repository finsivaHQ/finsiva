**UK Self Assessment** requires taxpayers to report their total income and allowable business expenses to calculate their precise tax bill.

### Important Deadlines:
- **5 October:** Deadline to register for Self Assessment if you became self-employed or had untaxed income in the previous tax year.
- **31 October:** Deadline for paper tax returns.
- **31 January:** Deadline for online tax returns and payment of tax due plus the first Payment on Account for the next year.
- **31 July:** Second Payment on Account deadline.
