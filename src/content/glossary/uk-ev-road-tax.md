## Definition

UK Electric Vehicle Road Tax (Vehicle Excise Duty / VED) is an annual tax paid to DVLA/HMRC for operating zero-emission electric cars, plug-in hybrids, and light goods vehicles on public UK roads.

## Explanation

Prior to 1 April 2025, zero-emission electric vehicles were exempt from annual VED (£0 rate). Under measures taking effect 1 April 2025:
- **First-Year Rate:** Brand new EVs pay a £10 first-year VED rate upon initial registration.
- **Standard Annual Rate:** From Year 2 onwards, EVs pay the standard annual VED rate of £190 per year.
- **Expensive Car Supplement:** Electric cars with a manufacturer list price (P11D value) over £40,000 pay an additional £410 annual surcharge for 5 years (Years 2 to 6).
