**Section 24** significantly alters the net profitability of residential buy-to-let properties held in individual names.

### Key Effects:
- Rental income is assessed on gross profits before deducting mortgage interest.
- Taxpayers may be pushed into higher tax brackets because gross rental income inflates adjusted net income.
- A 20% basic rate tax reduction is applied to finance costs at the end of the tax calculation.
