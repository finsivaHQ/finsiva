**Section 149** mandates employer payroll withholding in Pakistan. Employers project an employee's annual earnings, apply statutory FBR progressive tax slabs after accounting for exempt allowances and allowable deductions, and remit monthly tax deductions directly to the FBR by the 15th of each month.
