---
term: "Indonesia Income Tax"
category: "Income Tax"
definition: "Indonesia income tax (Pajak Penghasilan - PPh) is administered by the Direktorat Jenderal Pajak (DJP). Individual tax residents pay progressive personal income tax rates ranging from 5% to 35% across 5 tiers after deducting Non-Taxable Income (PTKP starting at IDR 54,000,000). Corporate entities pay a flat headline corporate tax rate of 22%."
