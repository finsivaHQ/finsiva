The **PAYE System** ensures employment tax is collected incrementally throughout the tax year rather than in one lump sum at year end.

### How Tax Codes Work:
- **1257L:** Standard tax code for an individual entitled to the full £12,570 Personal Allowance.
- **BR / D0 / D1:** Emergency or secondary tax codes applying tax at 20%, 40%, or 45% without allocating a personal allowance.
- **S-prefix (e.g. S1257L):** Identifies a Scottish taxpayer subject to Scottish income tax rates.
