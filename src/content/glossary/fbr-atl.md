The **Active Taxpayer List (ATL)** is published by the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) every Monday. It categorizes taxpayers into active filers and non-filers. Taxpayers on the ATL enjoy significantly lower withholding tax rates across banking transactions, property sales, vehicle token payments, and dividend payments.
