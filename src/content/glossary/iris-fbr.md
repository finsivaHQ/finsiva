The **FBR IRIS Portal** (<a href="https://iris.fbr.gov.pk" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">iris.fbr.gov.pk</a>) is the central electronic tax administration platform in Pakistan. It supports registration of National Tax Numbers (NTN), e-filing of annual income tax returns, wealth statements, sales tax returns (STRN), and active taxpayer verification.
